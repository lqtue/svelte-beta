difference matters more than the code does.

## The archive's own data — CC-BY-4.0

The work this project adds to the sheets: the georeferences and control points,
the place names and labels read off them and reviewed by hand, the traced
footprints, the gazetteer, and the catalogue metadata we wrote.

Archive Project and link to <https://maparchive.vn>. Full text:
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

This is the part volunteers built, and attribution is what they are owed.

## The scanned sheets — not ours to license

Each scan belongs to the institution that holds the paper and carries that
institution's own terms. The holding institution and the source URL are
recorded per sheet and shown on its catalogue page; check there, and with them,
before reusing an image. A permissive licence on our code and our annotations
says nothing about their scans.

## Third-party software and data

Dependencies keep their own licences (see `package.json` and the lockfile). The
basemap is built from OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors,
available under the Open Database License. The modern satellite imagery in the
header and the map viewer is Esri's and is attributed in the interface.
